# properties-y (properties-y.com)

Ultra-luxurious, distinctive corporate homepage for **properties-y**. 
Designed with a high-fashion editorial real estate layout acting as a gateway to premium Egyptian developments.

## Brand Positioning & Compliance
- **Positioning:** Independent Real Estate Consultancy & Strategic Advisory Partner
- **Brand Signature:** Featured prominently as "By properties-y"
- **Strict Compliance:** Zero gold policy (Calm / Midnight Luxury palette: pure whites, porcelain, stone grey `#F8F9FA`, and rich charcoal/slate `#1A202C`).

## Developer Portfolio
- **SODIC:** Signature communities in East Cairo and coastal sanctuaries.
- **Hassan Allam Properties:** Premium estates and fully finished residences.
- **Mountain View:** Innovative architecture and integrated city living.
- **Madinet Masr:** Expansive master-planned neighborhoods.
- **Qatari Diar (City Gate):** Elite golf communities and vast green landscapes.

## Key Features
- **Starts at top: 0:** No top fixed navbar; Hero leads directly with integrated editorial brand typography.
- **Strict Mobile Optimization:** Zero horizontal scroll, fluid responsive containers, stacked single-column mobile view, scaled headlines.
- **Global WhatsApp Integration:** Universal contact number `+201277752731` with floating icon and action CTAs.
- **Lead Capture & Success Modal:** Clean UI with split country-code selector and dynamic personalized confirmation modal.
- **Legal Compliance:** Standalone Privacy Policy (`privacy-policy.html`) and Terms & Conditions (`terms-conditions.html`).

## Local Development
Run the lightweight local server:
```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\serve.ps1 -Port 3000
```
Then visit [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000).
